A private CGBJ test reportedly showed movement and NPC interactions after the Xbox build leak. The Leonida project’s Discord server also disappeared.

TR Tech Media {{ page.date | date: "%-d %B %Y" }} {{ page.reading_time }} min read

An unofficial GTA 6 PC project called CGBJ reportedly reached in-game rendering on November 24, 2026. DSOGaming reported that a private test let a player move with a controller, interact with non-player characters and open the in-game menu. The tests followed Cyberleek’s claim that it released the main executable for a leaked Xbox Series build. Lighting and performance remained rough, and a stable, publicly playable PC build has yet to be independently verified. DSOGaming and PC Gamer.

What the GTA 6 PC rendering clips show

Getting beyond the menu means the test can draw parts of a scene and respond to player input. The clips shared on X show a game window with visible characters and scenery, alongside rendering glitches. The account onyx credited CGBJ in one post. Separately, chasm reported improvement in shaders and character hair. Shaders help determine how surfaces look on screen, including how they respond to light. Chasm said in a reply that they share updates from several projects. Their prediction of quick visual fixes was an estimate. Chasm’s posts and replies.

What Cyberleek claimed to leak and when

On November 19, Cyberleek claimed to have released a November Xbox development build labeled 1.0.2.1. The initial release was reported to omit the executable, the program that starts the game. On November 23, the leaker said that executable was out. PC Gamer reported the update, and community groups began showing early attempts to run the Xbox code on Windows. The build’s provenance remains unverified by Rockstar. In the reporting reviewed, Cyberleek is the name associated with the leak, while CGBJ and the other named projects are associated with the PC tests. VGC and PC Gamer.

How far CGBJ, VIctory and other projects got

DSOGaming described CGBJ as a private build based on work from VIctory and XCL. VIctory’s public status notes showed it could reach menus and load the opening area, where rendering was still incomplete. DSOGaming reported that XCL reached the menu, while Xbox2Windows and TuxBox had shown early frames. These are snapshots of separate tests, and the results can change as the projects update. The remaining milestones include rendering intact scenes, maintaining a stable frame rate and completing missions without crashes. DSOGaming’s project roundup and VIctory’s published status.

What happened to Leonida’s Discord server?

The Leonida community’s Discord server became inaccessible around 23:06 GMT on November 23, early on November 24 in Pakistan. Tarreo reported that the server had around 179,000 members shortly before it disappeared. The project’s GitHub repository also stopped loading, and its creator, known publicly as NotRexed, said they were stepping away and attributed the server removal to Rockstar. A GitHub 404 can also appear if a repository goes private. Public confirmation of who removed the server or whether a personal Discord account was deleted is still missing. Tarreo and Notebookcheck.

Take-Two had previously obtained a federal subpoena directing Discord to provide information identifying people suspected of copyright infringement. That August 31 order concerned identifying users. The circumstances of Leonida’s November disappearance remain unconfirmed. Federal court order.

Is there an official GTA 6 PC release date?

Rockstar’s November 19 launch announcement lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. As of November 24, Rockstar had not announced a GTA 6 PC release date in its official launch materials, and CGBJ remained a private test. Rockstar’s launch announcement and DSOGaming.
