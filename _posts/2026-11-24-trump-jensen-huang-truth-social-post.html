Trump posted an altered version of the 2024 Butler rally photo hours after Nvidia announced Jensen Huang’s reinstatement.

TR Tech Media {{ page.date | date: "%-d %B %Y" }} {{ page.reading_time }} min read

President Donald Trump shared a digitally altered image of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang on Truth Social at 11:32 a.m. EST on November 23, 2026, hours after Nvidia announced that Huang was returning as chief executive. The image shows Huang holding up a red bra in a reworked version of a photograph from Trump’s 2024 rally in Butler, Pennsylvania. Trump posted the image without a caption. Archived Truth Social post and The Washington Post.

What Trump’s Truth Social meme changed in the Butler photo

Associated Press photographer Evan Vucci took the original photograph on July 13, 2024. It shows Trump raising his fist as Secret Service agents surround him after the assassination attempt. An American flag is visible behind them. The version Trump shared substitutes Huang for Trump, adds the red bra to his raised hand, and replaces the U.S. flag with a black Nvidia banner. The image carries a @GRANDOLDMEMES watermark. AP’s account of the original photograph and archived meme.

The Washington Post read the red bra as an apparent reference to the controversy that preceded Huang’s resignation. The original image post offered no explanation for the prop. The Washington Post and Trump’s image post.

What Trump said earlier about Huang’s return to Nvidia

In a separate 9:07 a.m. EST Truth Social post, Trump called Nvidia’s decision the “correct and proper decision” and said the company should continue its review of compliance with U.S. export controls. In a CNBC interview Monday, Trump said he had spoken to Nvidia’s board and urged it to bring Huang back. Nvidia’s board said in its announcement that it had received input from senior U.S. officials before unanimously voting to reinstate him. Trump’s written statement, Reuters, and Nvidia’s announcement.

What comes next for Nvidia

The SEC filing dates the board’s decision to November 22. The announcement and Trump’s posts followed on November 23, when Nvidia shares closed up 31.5%. The U.S. Senate Banking Committee has scheduled Huang to testify on export controls on December 3, and Nvidia has created a board committee focused on national security and export controls. SEC filing, Senate hearing notice, and Nvidia’s announcement.
