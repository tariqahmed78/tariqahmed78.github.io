Huang’s return sent the AI chipmaker to $165.05 and lifted tech stocks. Nvidia’s board is continuing its investigation and adding new export-control oversight.

TR Tech Media {{ page.date | date: "%-d %B %Y" }} {{ page.reading_time }} min read

Nvidia reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as chief executive, president and a member of its board less than two weeks after announcing his departure. The board voted unanimously on Sunday, November 22, and Nvidia announced the decision the next morning. Nvidia stock (NASDAQ: NVDA) closed Monday at $165.05, up $39.52 or 31.5%. SEC filing and historical share prices.

Why Nvidia brought Jensen Huang back

Nvidia announced the original leadership transition on November 12 and named finance chief Colette Kress interim CEO. The board said it reinstated Huang after meeting with him and receiving input from senior U.S. government officials. The board said it was confident his return posed no national security risk. Kress remains executive vice president and chief financial officer, and Harvey C. Jones remains board chair. November 12 announcement and November 23 announcement.

Reuters reported that U.S. President Donald Trump had pressed for Huang to return. The company said its independent directors would continue investigating reports that Huang had been targeted by foreign intelligence. Nvidia also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee and began a search for a chief compliance officer. Reuters and Nvidia.

How Nvidia stock and Wall Street reacted

NVDA rose from Friday’s $125.53 close to $165.05 on Monday. That gain added nearly $1 trillion to Nvidia’s market value, according to Reuters. Shares still finished below their level before Huang’s resignation. Historical share prices and Reuters market coverage.

How Nvidia’s stock rally spread to the Nasdaq

Nvidia’s size gives it an outsized influence on market-cap-weighted indexes. Huang’s return also offered some continuity for an AI chip business that many data center operators depend on. The Nasdaq’s 6.5% gain reflected a wider rebound in AI stocks. U.S. stock futures had already been rising before the reinstatement announcement, and gains extended to the small-cap Russell 2000. The Associated Press and Reuters.

What remains open after Huang’s return

Huang’s reinstatement settles who leads Nvidia. The independent investigation, the search for a compliance chief, and congressional scrutiny of chip exports are still ongoing. The board’s next disclosures can show how the new committee will oversee export controls and what the independent directors find. For the business itself, Nvidia’s data center sales and customer spending will show whether demand is keeping pace with expectations. Nvidia’s governance announcement and Reuters.
