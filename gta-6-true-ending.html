How to Get the GTA 6 True Ending and Unlock Two Years Later

Check the best ending requirements before Protective Custody, then make the right choices at the bridge.

TR Tech Media Updated 24 Nov 2026 5 min read

Spoiler warning. This guide explains how the GTA 6 story ends.

Jason can get arrested and you can still unlock the GTA 6 true ending. For the Two Years Later reunion, aim for Hot Together, keep both Criminal Profiles at Professional, and put $2 million into BuckMe Family Savings before Protective Custody. Choose Shoot when Méndez offers the deal, then Swim for It at the bridge and Exit when prompted. This is the route documented by PC Gamer and GTABase.

GTA 6 best ending checklist

Work on the relationship, both profiles and savings as you play. All three take longer to fix than the final button prompts.

  1. Get four hearts. Build Jason and Lucia’s relationship until the rating reads Hot Together. Spend time together and reply to each other’s messages.
  2. Check both Criminal Profiles. Aim for Professional on Jason and Lucia individually. Skip unnecessary crimes and fights as you play.
  3. Get the joint savings balance to $2 million. Open BuckMe and verify the figure on the Family Savings screen.
  4. Keep a manual backup. Use Pause → Game → Save Game and save in a separate slot before committing to Protective Custody. If Abe offers Postpone and you still need to prepare, take it.

Professional on both profiles is the documented target for this route. It’s safer than assuming a more aggressive profile qualifies. PC Gamer’s ending breakdown and GTA Guide’s pre-finale save notes cover those checks.

How to put $2 million in BuckMe Family Savings

If you’re carrying pocket cash, deposit it into Checking at an ATM first. On the in-game phone, open BuckMe, select Checking, then use Buck Yourself to transfer money into Family Savings. Confirm the transfer and read the joint balance again. Jason and Lucia have separate Checking balances, and either character can contribute to the same shared account. GTA BOOM’s BuckMe walkthrough.

Family Savings transfers are one-way. Leave the money you need for gear and story expenses in Checking. If you’re just short of the ending target, check where the last payout landed before starting the mission.

Choose Shoot, then Swim for It at the bridge

  1. When Méndez offers a deal during Protective Custody, select Shoot to stay on the route to The End of the Road. Taking his deal leads to a separate ending before the bridge. GTABase’s Protective Custody walkthrough.
  2. At the bridge roadblock, choose Swim for It with Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox. Follow the displayed prompt if your controls differ. Output Lag’s bridge choice guide.
  3. Listen for Jason telling Lucia to keep her weapon down. If the game then shows Exit, select it promptly. The dialogue tells you that you’re on the unarmed bridge route. The reunion is revealed later. PC Gamer’s guide to the Exit prompt.

How to tell if you got the Two Years Later ending

In the survival branch, Lucia makes it into the water while Jason stays behind and is arrested. Keep watching. The ordinary good ending shows Tomás visiting Jason in prison. In the reunion epilogue, the story jumps ahead two years, Lucia works with Abe to get Jason released, and the three leave together. That family reunion is what players call the true or best ending. GTABase’s finale walkthrough.

Why you got the good ending instead of the true ending

If the credits ended with Tomás visiting Jason in prison, you got Jason Takes the Fall. You made it through the bridge sequence. Start by checking where the $2 million was held and when you transferred it.

  1. Open Family Savings in BuckMe. The $2 million target refers to the shared balance. Checking balances and cash on hand are separate.
  2. Check your pre-finale save. PC Gamer documents the reunion with the money deposited before Protective Custody. GTABase also reports a successful route by topping up in free roam after the credits and then replaying the finale. If a replay still gives you the prison visit, reload the earlier manual save and verify the balance there. PC Gamer’s ending requirements and GTABase’s replay notes.

If Jason and Lucia died before either got to the water, you’re troubleshooting a different outcome. Check both Criminal Profiles and whether Lucia took the Exit prompt. Our Failed Escape breakdown explains how that can happen even after choosing Swim for It.

GTA 6 true ending FAQs

Does Jason have to avoid arrest to get the best ending?

No. His arrest happens on the reunion route too. His release and reunion with Lucia and Tomás come in the epilogue.

Can I get the true ending just by choosing Swim for It?

No. Your preparation changes the outcome, and you should also take the Exit prompt if it appears. If you want to see what happens when the couple drives through the blockade, use the separate GTA 6 bad ending guide.
