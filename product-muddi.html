One toolkit. Four workspaces.

Make room for what you create.

01 / VIDEO

Keep the moments. Reduce the size.

Compress videos to H.264 MP4, trim unwanted sections, and compare quality settings. Choose a preset or a target size, then review the result. Actual sizes and supported exports depend on your media and device.

02 / PHOTO

A new look for your pictures.

Make smaller JPEGs, crop, rotate, flip and resize images. Remove edge-connected backgrounds, repair cutouts with erase and restore brushes, and add a color, gradient or replacement photo.

03 / AUDIO

Find your sound.

Extract a video soundtrack to M4A, or open an audio file in the separate enhancer. Preview loudness, bass and voice-pitch settings before saving a new M4A copy.

04 / DOCUMENTS & UTILITIES

Turn scattered pages into a document.

Import or photograph pages, adjust corners and filters, and export PDF, JPEG or PNG. Build PDFs with up to 30 images. Utilities include two-video batches and copies of accessible WhatsApp statuses.

Clear expectations.

Background removal works best when the subject and edge background are distinct; complex scenes may need manual repair. The scanner uses manual corners and does not offer OCR or searchable PDFs. Status Saver cannot recover expired or inaccessible statuses and is independent of WhatsApp and Meta.
