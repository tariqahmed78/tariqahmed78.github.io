App Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — OCTOBER 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Fiber Router Manager (Package Name: com.tariqinvents.routerapp), published by TR Tech Invents ("we", "our", "us") on the Google Play Store, handles network and device information[cite: 1]. This document is hosted at https://trtechmedia.com/privacy-policy.html. Looking for the privacy policy for this website instead? See it here.

Notice Regarding Accounts & User Profiles: Fiber Router Manager functions strictly as a direct hardware management utility. The App does not feature account registration, does not create or manage user profiles, and does not collect or store user accounts or personal profiles.

01Information We Access and Handle

Local Network & Router Data LOCAL • EPHEMERAL

To inspect and manage your local fiber router hardware (including Huawei ONT, ZTE/China Mobile, and Realtek Boa models) and discover connected clients, the App inspects[cite: 2, 7]:

  • Gateway Addresses: Local default gateway IPs (such as 192.168.100.1 or 192.168.1.1) used to reach your router's administration interface over your local Wi-Fi[cite: 2].
  • Connected Device Endpoints: Local IP addresses, MAC addresses, connection interface tags, and hostnames of devices connected to your LAN[cite: 2].
  • Wireless Configurations: Wi-Fi Network Names (SSID), BSSIDs, frequency bands (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz), and Wi-Fi channels[cite: 2].
  • Optical & Hardware Diagnostics: Optical Rx/Tx power levels (dBm), PON link states, and port link speeds[cite: 5, 7].

Ephemeral Guarantee: All local network and router communication is processed ephemerally on-device inside volatile RAM. It is never logged on remote servers, never monetized, and never leaves your local area network[cite: 2].

Router Administration Credentials LOCAL • ENCRYPTED

The administrative username and password you input to authenticate against your local router interface are used solely to execute authorized administrative commands (e.g., changing Wi-Fi credentials, viewing optical levels, or applying MAC filtering rules)[cite: 2].

Credentials are stored exclusively on your physical device within Android's hardware-backed EncryptedSharedPreferences using AES-256 GCM encryption. Your credentials are never uploaded to remote cloud databases[cite: 2].

Diagnostics & Stability Data ANONYMOUS TELEMETRY

To ensure application performance and identify firmware-specific bugs, the App uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash stack traces and technical device diagnostics.
  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable app interaction metrics to guide technical optimizations.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Basic configuration parameters for remote version checks and maintenance notices.

Advertising & Mobile Identifiers ADVERTISING

The App displays advertisements via Google AdMob[cite: 1, 2]. AdMob may process the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and coarse location parameters to serve non-intrusive ads and manage privacy preferences through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

02Permissions & System Disclosures

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required by the Android OS to invoke system Wi-Fi scanning APIs and retrieve Wi-Fi SSIDs/BSSIDs. Fiber Router Manager does not track, record, log, or transmit your physical GPS location[cite: 1, 2].
  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES): Required on Android 13+ to scan and interface with local networking equipment without accessing location data.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver optional local network monitoring alerts and service notices.

03How We Use Information

  • To interface directly with your local router gateway for Wi-Fi management and diagnostic readings[cite: 2].
  • To detect connected devices on your LAN and execute one-tap MAC filter block/unblock actions[cite: 2].
  • To conduct localized network throughput and latency tests.
  • To detect software errors and maintain compatibility across diverse Android device configurations.

04Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted SDKs that process technical information according to their independent privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system integration • Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config): Diagnostics & version checks • Firebase Terms
  • Google AdMob & UMP SDK: In-app advertising and privacy consent • AdMob Terms[cite: 2]
  • Square, Inc. (OkHttp): Secure local HTTP/HTTPS socket networking client • Square Privacy

05Data Retention & Erasure

  • No Server-Side Accounts: Because the App does not maintain user accounts or profiles, we do not store personal account databases.
  • Device Storage Purge: All locally cached router credentials and settings can be erased permanently at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings > Apps > Fiber Router Manager > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application[cite: 1, 2].
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymous telemetry stored within Google Firebase is automatically purged on a rolling 14-month retention cycle.

06Security Safeguards

We implement strict cryptographic protections to safeguard your hardware control channel:

  • Local router credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore-backed AES-256 GCM storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • External network requests to Google Firebase and AdMob are strictly enforced over Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Router management calls are isolated to your local area network subnet[cite: 2].

07Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD)

Under international privacy directives:

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary or valuable consideration.
  • Consent Controls: You can manage or revoke advertising personalization at any time through system settings or the in-app consent menu.
  • Right to Know & Delete: Users can confirm that no personal profile records are held on our systems and purge all device-stored data via Android settings.

08Children's Privacy (Google Play Families Policy)

Fiber Router Manager is designed for network administrators and general audiences managing physical fiber routing hardware[cite: 1, 2]. The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).

In accordance with the Google Play Families Policy, our declared target audience is 18 years of age and older[cite: 13]. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information through the App, contact us for prompt removal.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory modifications. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated "LAST UPDATED" timestamp.
