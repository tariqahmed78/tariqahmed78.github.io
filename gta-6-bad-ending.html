How to Get the GTA 6 Bad Ending

TR Tech Media 24 Nov 2026 4 min read

Spoiler warning. This guide explains how the GTA 6 story ends.

To get the GTA 6 bad ending at the bridge, choose No Surrender in The End of the Road. Press R2 on PS5 or RT on Xbox with the default controls. Jason and Lucia both die on this route. A high relationship rating and $2 million in savings can change what you see in the credits, but they cannot save them after this choice. Button prompts from Output Lag and ending variants from PC Gamer.

How to unlock No Surrender in GTA 6

  1. Make a separate manual save. If you want to explore other branches later, save before Protective Custody. Keep that slot when the game creates newer checkpoints.
  2. Stay on the bridge route. Keep Jason and Lucia together and choose Shoot when Méndez offers his deal in Protective Custody. A breakup can send the story to a different location, and accepting his deal ends it on a separate branch. GTABase’s Protective Custody guide and PC Gamer’s route breakdown.
  3. Reach the roadblock. Continue through The End of the Road until Jason and Lucia are stopped on the bridge.
  4. Choose No Surrender. Read the label on the screen and press R2 or RT when the choice appears.

What happens in the No Surrender ending?

Jason drives into the blockade with Lucia beside him. The car gets through the barricade, but the deputies kill them both. The credits then show their son, Tomás, growing up with Lucia’s relatives. That last scene explains why this ending is about what he loses as much as what happens on the bridge. GTABase’s finale walkthrough.

There is a reported credits variant if the couple put $2 million into Family Savings before Protective Custody. Their sacrifice plays out the same way, but the money reaches the family raising Tomás. The extra scene can look like a reward for preparing well while still taking the tragic route. PC Gamer’s No Surrender savings variant.

Failed Escape and Méndez’s Deal

Failed Escape after choosing Swim for It

Swim for It can still turn fatal. GTABase documents the failed escape on saves with a Psycho or Irredeemable profile. PC Gamer also documents a fatal result after the timed Exit prompt expires on a violent-profile route. If you chose Swim for It and still lost both characters, check your earlier profile and whether Lucia exited the car. GTABase’s failed escape entry and PC Gamer’s bridge guide.

Méndez’s Deal before the bridge

During Protective Custody, wait for the Take Deal prompt instead of choosing Shoot. Accepting it sends the story down an earlier route that ends with Méndez betraying the couple. Choose this branch if you want to see the swamp ending. For the bridge ending, take the Shoot route. GTABase’s guide to the deal.

Can you try the bad ending and then get the true ending?

Yes. GTABase reports that after the bridge credits, the game returns you to before the final mission so you can try a different bridge choice. Keep your earlier manual save for changing decisions made during Protective Custody or checking whether your savings qualified before that mission. GTABase’s post-credits notes.
