Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra and Galaxy S27 Pro batteries have been pictured in a new certification

4 min read Published Aug 5, 2026

Samsung Galaxy S27 battery certification details

Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra and Galaxy S27 Pro batteries have been pictured in a new certification. The Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra have been seen making the certification rounds, and their batteries have shown up in the South Korea safety department database, complete with images.

The battery models which have been seen are the BS957CBY and the BEBBS958DBY, which line up with the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra (SM-S957 and SM-S958) model part numbers. Both models are made in China by CosMX Zhuhai, but capabilities aren't clear in the photos. Thankfully, we already know them.

Battery capacity and charging information

The capacity first leaked in July via China 3C certification, and then again in September. The S27 Ultra capacity is rated at 5,534 mAh, which typically translates into 5,700 mAh, while the S27 Pro is rated at 5,087 mAh, likely 5,200 mAh typically. Both phones are known for 60W wired charging, just like the S26 Ultra. If those figures are right, the S27 Ultra will have 700 mAh more than the S26 Ultra.

Samsung battery strategy and launch timing

It has been known that Samsung has been stuck with 5,000 mAh for years. But now we have seen some reports that Samsung was testing a 7,000 mAh prototype before settling on a smaller cell to keep the S Pen. It is still not clear when exactly the S27 will launch, but recent reports suggest Samsung will launch it in the January or February timeframe, though it is still unclear.
